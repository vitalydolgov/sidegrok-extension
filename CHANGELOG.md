# Changelog

## 1.3.2 — 2026-10-26

Choosing a bot opens its conversation without reloading the page.

## 1.3.1 — 2026-10-25

The bot picker stays disabled until the bot list has loaded.

## 1.3.0 — 2026-10-24

The bot picker now groups bots into sections, with pinned bots listed first.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-22

The drawer now has a bot picker at the bottom so you can switch between your Grok bots. Hide the panel under the extension’s Preferences in about:addons.

## 1.1.1 — 2026-10-18

The sidebar launcher icon now follows light and dark mode while Firefox is running.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-07

Opt+Cmd+A on macOS, or Ctrl+Alt+A on Windows and Linux, shows or hides the drawer. Turn the shortcut off under the extension’s Preferences in about:addons.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-03

A sidebar button opens Grok. Opening the sidebar returns to the last page you had open there.
